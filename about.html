Artisan Lineage

A Commitment to Saddle Stitching

Bagquorix was established with a singular, unwavering mission: to rescue the fading art of manual saddle stitching and introduce it to luxury leather bags. In a world increasingly saturated by quick synthetic threads and mass-processed glued linings, we dedicate our workshop to hand-saddled hides and custom patterns. We believe a bag handle is not simply a carry strap; it is a representation of shoulder comfort, weight distribution, and master leathercraft.

Each leather panel is harvested individually inside sustainable tanneries in Alsace, France. Once received at our New York showroom, the hide is hand-clicked, hand-basted, and hot-waxed entirely by hand. Organic wax polishes use natural beeswaxes and mineral dyes instead of cheap synthetic glazes, protecting hide breathability and edge finishes. It is this minute attention to microscopic details that allows our master weavers to sustain a Lifetime Quality Pledge.

Our Three Workshop Pillars

01. Saddle Stitching

We draw stitch paths manually under strict templates, forming tailored outlines.

02. Hide Clicking

Stitching pocket walls, packing interlining canvas, and welting handle straps slowly.

03. Solid Brass Hardware

Applying metallic buckles, testing zipper pulls, and hot pressing gold initials.

The Saddler Future

As Bagquorix looks to the upcoming decade, we remain anchored in our slow-craft principles. We maintain a small workshop of certified weavers, limiting our global output to a few hundred leather sets per year. This exclusivity ensures that every client registered receives the undivided focus and care of our press masters. Bagquorix is a lifetime commitment to hide clicks, saddle stitches, and heritage leathercraft preservation.
